Asking for the camera…

Trigger point and choose; hold the other trigger for a second thing: the distance
Grip hold the model; both grips: size it and turn it
Grip + trigger of one hand: fine, it moves a fifth as far
Stick up and down: bigger, smaller; pressed: real size, in front of you
A or X held: cut it open along your hand; tapped: whole again
B or Y see-through
